Our story

Grameen means “of the village”

It started in my grandmother’s kitchen in Bundelkhand, where ghee was made every Sunday and the mango pickle jars sat on the terrace for most of May.

We work with 40 families who still make food this way. We pay them fairly, test every batch, and put nothing in the jar that was not in hers.

With care, Lakshmi. Founder
