Our story

Grameen means “of the village”

It started in my grandmother’s kitchen in Bundelkhand, where ghee was made every Sunday and the mango pickle jars sat on the terrace for most of May.

We work with 40 families who still make food this way. We pay them fairly, test every batch, and put nothing in the jar that was not in hers.

With care, Lakshmi. Founder

Dadi’s mango pickle

Handwritten, 1962 · Makes one big jar

Cut the mangoes with the stone in, never peeled. Salt them overnight, dry them under the May sun for two days, then fold in the spices and the mustard oil warmed until it just smokes.

Fill the jar to the neck. Leave it on the terrace for six weeks and stir it every Sunday. We still make it this way.

“Never rush the sun, beta.”
