Dal tadka with a spoon of ghee

[ Hero image: dal in a brass bowl ]

Dal is the easiest dish to make well and the easiest to make forgettable. The last spoon of ghee decides which one you get. [Placeholder body copy]

What you need

  • 1 cup toor dal, washed
  • 2 tbsp Grameen A2 Desi Ghee
  • 1 tsp cumin, 2 dry red chillies, 4 garlic cloves
Heat the ghee until the cumin sizzles, then pour it over the dal and cover at once.
